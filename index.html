    Leaderboard rules

    1. How your wagers count. The board runs on a weighted wager, not your raw wager amount. Games are weighted by their RTP:
      • A game at 96% RTP counts in full — $1 wagered is $1 towards the board.
      • A game above 96% RTP counts less, scaled to its RTP. For example a 99% RTP game counts about 25%, and a 99.6% RTP game about 10%.
      • Sports bets count triple — $1 wagered counts as $3.
    2. Who counts. Only players signed up to Rainbet under code YETISLOTS appear on the board.
    3. The period. Calendar month — 1st to the last day of the month. Resets at midnight UK time on the 1st.
    4. The prizes. $250 shared across the top 5 wagerers — 1st $100 · 2nd $60 · 3rd $40 · 4th $30 · 5th $20.
    5. Ranking. Highest weighted wager takes 1st, and so on down. A place with nobody in it pays nothing and shows a dash.
    6. When it updates. Refreshed automatically every midnight UK time, straight from Rainbet's official affiliate data. The board can be a few hours behind your latest play.
    7. Final standings. Totals are taken from Rainbet at the moment the period closes, and those figures are final. Closed periods stay on the site under Past winners.
    8. Getting paid. Winners are announced live on stream at the start of each month.
